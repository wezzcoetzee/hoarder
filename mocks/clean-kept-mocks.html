My pick: A

Smallest change and it can't lose work: the grey rows reuse the existing panel, and git's own refusal guards dirty and locked worktrees. If you want those cleanable too, C adds --force with a red warning without an extra click. B's extra step is the safest feel but adds a state to the panel for every grey row.
